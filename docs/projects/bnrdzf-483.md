This mod fully replaces the soundset of both the WiKo cabcar and the n-Wagen.
For more details see the features below.
And with that: Allzeit gute Fahrt!

## Features

Bnrdzf 483.0:
- Running sounds (int & ext)
- Redone flange simulation and sounds (int & ext)
- Cab wind noise (int)
- New set of joint sounds (int & ext)
- Internal brake valve sound simulation and sounds (int)
- Randomized brake squeal sounds (int & ext)
- New Drehfalttüren sounds with distance effect inside (int & ext)
- Horn (int & ext)
- New soundset for switches, levers and buttons (int)
- PZB & Sifa alerts (int)
- Cab door & window sounds (int)
- Replaced wiper sounds
- Various adjustments in sound mixes and attenuations

n-Wagen:
- Running sounds (int & ext)
- Redone flange simulation and sounds (int & ext)
- New set of joint sounds (int & ext)
- Randomized brake squeal sounds (int & ext)
- New Drehfalttüren sounds with distance effect inside (int & ext)
- Various adjustments in sound mixes and attenuations
