## Short foreword
A huge thank you to everyone involved in the charity stream hosted by TSP, Trainsa and JM Simulation!
Be it that you were active (or will be) in the stream chat or even donated.
As the BPH we thought as a halfway goal of 1.000€ (a sum we thought was big) we will release 2 mods...
We were proven wrong, in the first stream alone we already collected over 3.500€ and this goes all to a good cause!

Now the year 2025 comes to an end.
It was a very good year. We released many mods that you folks likes, we shared many nice moments together
and despite having some problemes here and there all in all the year was great!

### LA Fix
Whilst being a small mod this mod enhances the driving experience in germany alot!
What it does you might ask?
This mod replaces the temporary speed restriction signs with new models fitting more to the real counterpart.

Download is available on Train Sim Community: [LA Fix (Temporary Speedrestrictions)](https://www.trainsimcommunity.com/mods/c3-train-sim-world/c19-patches/i7143-langsamfahrstellen-fix-temporary-speed-restrictions-fix)

### TRAXX AC2 Regio-/Fernverkehr-Pack
A highly anticipated mod from last roadmap...
`@Mabitze` finished this mod in godspeed that we were able to set it as one of the donation goals to hit!
And here it is, featuring 3 subvariants of the 146 in a total of over 15 repaints and 3 operators (Regio, FV and Metronom)
Futhermore many textures got replaced, subclass specific changes to the model were made and the Bombardier FIS system was implemented and is functional!

Download available on Train Sim Community: [TRAXX AC2 Regio-/Fernverkehr-Pack](https://www.trainsimcommunity.com/mods/c3-train-sim-world/c18-pak-file-skins/c67-european/i7142-traxx-ac2-regio-fernverkehr-pack)
