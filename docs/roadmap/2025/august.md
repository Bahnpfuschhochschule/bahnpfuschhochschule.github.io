### BR 218 Livery & Enhancement Pack
Probably the most favorite german diesel locomotive of many finally got the love it deserved...
This pack has been in development for a while now but we wanted to keep it a secret until now and we really saw the
excitement once we showed it! -Surprise successful
This pack mainly started off as a repaint pack porting over the repaints created by `@Eric_0815`, member of the RDF group.
Besides the repaints it will also host many other changes:

- New soundset replacing misc sounds & finally a two tone horn
- Variants where the snowplow is not mounted
- A version with working GSM-R
- New textures like lights and indicators, as well as a LED variant
- Accessible machine room!
- Many small details that can be changed via click-spots (Like ZG2-Boards, depot of the loco and many other things)
- Different weathering presets

## In production - Release TBA
Now the projects where a release date is yet unknown / tba:

### Salzburg - Rosenheim Scenery Enhancements
The already released mod by our member `@FreddieTheShepherd` is getting yet another update adding even more details to the route and more things to to look at whilst driving!

Updates to the Salzburg Area include:
- Static train formation displays
- Enhanced trackside noise barriers
- Overhead wires of the electric busses
- Added ÖBB sign to all S-Bahn stations
- Expanded walkable area around the Salzach
- Changes to the station "Salzburg Lieferung" when 2024 pack is chosen
- Height of OHLE changed
- New water textures
- Changes at the Europark
- New station annoucements
- Redone airplane sound after bug reports

#### Scenery+
General changes to scenery like vegetation and placement of houses and other objects to make it as close to reallife as possible
Currently reworked is the section from Rosenheim to the Inn-Bridge and the Bahnpark at Freilassing

### Update to the Eurofima Pack
The already released Eurofima Pack will recieve an update, making it standalone from the 101 DLC.
Furthermore the update will include more formations to choose from, more coach types (Bpmz, WLABmz) and liveries from the 90s!
The pack will be done in collaboration with `@Swing Swong`. Thanks for helping!

### TRAXX AC2 Regio-/Fernverkehr-Pack
A mod started by `@Mabitze` which will bring you the .3 and .5 subclass of the BR146!
This mod is fully compatible with the BR146 soundmod.
The mod will bring the BR 146.5 in Metronom and FV livery and the 146.3 in DB Regio red.
Besides that the cabs have been changed to fit the subclasses and have been fitted with things like:

- New MFD/MTD
- New EBuLa
- Placeable objects in the cab
- Working FIS controller on the right side
- Different repaints for the DB Regio variant
