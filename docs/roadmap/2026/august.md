Hello everyone!

## What happend lately?
things have been a little quieter around BPH recently....
Over the past months, our focus has shifted towards other projects and, of course, real life and private matters.
As much as we love working on TSW, sometimes other stuff simply takes priority.

On top of that, we also had some internal changes and disagreements within the team,
which resulted in `@FreddieTheShepherd`, `@Prototype001` and `@Manny` leaving BPH for a while.
Thankfully, things have calmed down since then.

`@FreddieTheShepherd` and `@Prototype001` have both returned to BPH and
are once again working with us.
However, they may still release some of their work individually under their own names, rather than exclusively through BPH ^^
`@Manny` is currently no longer part of the BPH team.

## New member!
